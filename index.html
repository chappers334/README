Start a test

Take a photo of the watch to begin.

  1. Tap Photograph watch below (or the upload button for a photo already in your gallery). The AI recognises the brand and model, then asks you for the job number.
  2. Wind the watch and keep it running. It doesn’t need to show the right time.
  3. Once a day, tap Photograph watch and take a sharp, square-on photo of the dial with the seconds hand visible.
  4. The first photo is the starting point and counts as 0 s. Each later photo compares how far the watch moved since the previous photo with how much time really passed, so you see the seconds gained or lost each day.
  5. After a week you have the average seconds per day and the total gained or lost.
