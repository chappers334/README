Add a watch

  1. Set the watch roughly to the right time (within a few minutes) and keep it running.
  2. Once a day, tap Photograph watch and take a sharp, square-on photo of the dial with the seconds hand visible.
  3. Tap the hands on the photo. The app compares the watch with your phone’s network-synced clock at the moment of the photo.
  4. After a week you have the average seconds per day and the total gained or lost.
